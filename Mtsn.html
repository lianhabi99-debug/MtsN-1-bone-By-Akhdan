About Us

Welcome to MTsN 1 Bone

MTsN 1 Bone is a premier Islamic secondary school located in Bone Regency, South Sulawesi. Established with a vision to create a generation of excellence, our school integrates academic achievement with strong Islamic character education.

With a rich history spanning decades, we have consistently produced outstanding graduates who excel in academics, leadership, and Islamic values. Our dedicated teachers, modern facilities, and supportive learning environment make MTsN 1 Bone the ideal place for students to grow and thrive.

We believe in nurturing the whole student — intellectually, spiritually, and socially — preparing them to become future leaders who are knowledgeable, faithful, and beneficial to society.

Our Direction

Vision & Mission

Our guiding principles that shape every aspect of education at MTsN 1 Bone

Our Vision

"To become a leading Islamic educational institution that produces generations of excellence in academic achievement, strong Islamic character, leadership skills, technological literacy, and environmental awareness, based on the values of Ahlussunnah Wal Jama'ah."

Our Mission

Providing quality academic education based on the national curriculum integrated with Islamic values
Developing students' character through Islamic teachings and moral education
Fostering leadership skills and entrepreneurial spirit among students
Integrating digital literacy and technology in the learning process
Building environmental awareness and love for nature
Creating a conducive, inclusive, and joyful learning environment
